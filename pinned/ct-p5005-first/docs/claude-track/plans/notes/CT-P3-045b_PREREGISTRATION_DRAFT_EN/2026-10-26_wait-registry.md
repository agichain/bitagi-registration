## 1. Two facts behind most rows

1. **No CURRENT Heart of this campaign during a core `Wait`.** Every core `Wait::*` comes after `outcome()` (L/machine.rs:1306-1308). Any inclusion or project birth makes the candidate `Done` (L/machine.rs:1408-1423). So the core waits (W21–W31) are pre-birth.
2. **Refusals hide `Done`, and step 9 needs `Done`.** Open locks, CAMPAIGN_STOPs, full tables and node checks refuse before the phase (L/machine.rs:148-240). Step 9 counts only `Verdict::Done` (H/finality.rs:226-241). So each such refusal also stalls W1, and through W1 it stalls W2.

## 2. Registry

Columns:
- **Start (anchor):** what enters the wait, and the height it is measured from.
- **End:** the completion predicate and its boundary. "**no exit**" means none was found.
- **Reorg:** whether the wait is recomputed after a reorganization.
- **Blocks:** the writes and acts it holds. "BEAT-if-shared" says whether it would hold a BEAT write if BEAT rows share the ledger.
- **CURRENT:** whether a Born Heart of the campaign can exist during the wait.
- **Exit today:** the continuation path that exists now, or BLOCKED.
